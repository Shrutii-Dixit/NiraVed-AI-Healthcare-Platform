About

NiraVed is a student research prototype exploring heart disease and stroke risk from structured inputs. It is not a medical diagnostic service.

Modules: Heart risk (UCI Cleveland), Stroke risk (Kaggle), CAD risk (Z-Alizadeh Sani). Models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM with leakage-safe CV, SMOTE, SHAP explainability, calibration.
