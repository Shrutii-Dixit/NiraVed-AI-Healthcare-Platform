## Team guidance

Before adding a paper, search this document and the team's shared reference list by title, DOI, and author to avoid duplicate entries. Prefer the published paper or an authoritative repository record over a secondary summary. Record the study's actual population, task, data, and reported limitations. Do not convert association into causality, claim its result as a NiraVed result, or fill unknown citation fields from memory. If a faculty-approved table format exists, preserve it and extend it only with agreement.
